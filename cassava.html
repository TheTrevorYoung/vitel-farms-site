PRODUCTION DISCIPLINE

Grow a repeatable system, not just a crop.

Vitel’s production strategy is built around land and acreage planning, planting material, field preparation, crop calendars, labor, input control, crop monitoring, harvest scheduling, measured yield, post-harvest handling and field-level cost accounting.

The objective is reliable production that can meet real market requirements—not production for its own sake.

WHERE VALUE CAN MOVE

Six levers worth measuring.

01

Planting material

Clean, appropriate and productive varieties matched to farmer and market needs.

02

Agronomy

Practical field management that improves stand establishment and crop performance.

03

Harvest timing

Coordinate maturity, labor, transport and buyers so roots move quickly after harvest.

04

Aggregation

Combine supply where useful to improve reliability and reduce fragmented transactions.

05

Processing

Convert roots into products when demand, quality, conversion rates and margins support it.

06

Data

Measure cost, yield, losses, buyer, price and payment instead of managing by anecdote.

CIRCULAR VALUE

Use more of what the crop produces.

Cassava processing creates peels, pulp, wastewater and other residues. Vitel will explore productive uses only where they are safe, technically sound, legally compliant and commercially sensible.

Historical TSEAI work on agricultural residues and energy informs that curiosity, but no current waste-to-energy facility or project is being claimed.
