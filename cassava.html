BASELINE NOTE

Use the right denominator.

The 2024 Sierra Leone agricultural survey reports 2,855,435.8 tonnes of cassava production. Its 11.8 t/ha yield aligns with the 242,380 ha harvested area, not the larger planted-area figure.

PRODUCTION DISCIPLINE

Grow a repeatable cassava system.

The workstream focuses on planting material, agronomy, crop timing, measured yields, harvest planning and cost control.

The goal is reliable production for real market demand.

WHERE VALUE CAN MOVE

Six levers worth measuring.

01

Planting material

Clean, appropriate and productive varieties matched to farmer and market needs.

02

Agronomy

Practical field management that improves stand establishment and crop performance.

03

Harvest timing

Coordinate maturity, labor, transport and buyers so roots move quickly after harvest.

04

Aggregation

Combine supply where useful to improve reliability and reduce fragmented transactions.

05

Processing

Convert roots into products when demand, quality, conversion rates and margins support it.

06

Data

Measure cost, yield, losses, buyer, price and payment instead of managing by anecdote.

CASSAVA AMBITION

Around #21 today. #1 remains a long-horizon national ambition.

FAOSTAT’s 2024 comparable production series places Sierra Leone around #21 globally at about 2.86 million tonnes. Vitel retains the ambition to contribute to a Sierra Leonean cassava system capable of world-leading performance.

This is a cassava-specific national ambition—not Vitel Farms’ company identity or a near-term forecast.
