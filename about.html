ABOUT VITEL FARMS

Old roots. A sharper agricultural mission.

Vitel Farms is a Sierra Leonean agribusiness initiative focused on building commercially viable cassava production and value chains.

THE LINEAGE

From TSEAI to Vitel Farms.

Vitel’s institutional history reaches back to Tseai Energy Unlimited (TSEAI), an earlier Sierra Leone-focused agricultural and renewable-energy initiative whose archived records place its origins around 2009.

Historical records document work around agricultural processing, farmer relationships, the Kambia/Mile 18 area, and collaboration with University of Maryland researchers on productive uses for agricultural processing waste.

The earlier project later paused as funding ran out. Vitel Farms emerged in a later phase with cassava at the center of the business model.

History standard: dates, assets and historical relationships are being reconciled from archived records before they are used as current operating claims.

WHY CASSAVA

A crop big enough to matter nationally.

Cassava is deeply embedded in Sierra Leone’s food system and rural economy, with more than 300,000 agricultural holdings reporting cassava cultivation in the 2024 national survey.

It also creates multiple market pathways—from fresh roots and leaves to gari, flour, starch, feed and other products. That combination makes it a powerful platform for building agricultural capability.
