FOUNDER & ROOTS

Built from work that started in Kambia.

Vitel Farms is led by founder Trevor Young, who began Tseai Energy Unlimited (TEU) around 2009 as a Sierra Leone-focused agriculture and renewable-energy venture. Earlier work in Kambia explored farming, processing, farmer markets and ways to create more value from agricultural production. Vitel is the next chapter: cassava first, commercially disciplined, and clear about what is historical, current or still being verified. Read the Vitel story →

FARMER NETWORK PROTOCOL

25 verified records. Then 50. Then 100.

These are verification milestones, not current farmer counts.

25

Build the first usable verified cohort and test the record standard.

50

Expand only after the first cohort produces reliable field and market information.

100

Scale the network when verification, support and market pathways can keep up.
