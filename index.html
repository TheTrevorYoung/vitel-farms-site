CURRENT FLAGSHIP MISSION

Cassava first. One mission, full focus.

Sierra Leone produces about 2.86 million tonnes and ranks around #22 globally. Vitel’s current mission is to help build the systems that can move the country toward #1.

The rank is a national benchmark. Vitel’s own contribution will be reported separately through verified farmers, hectares, yields, tonnes, processing and markets.
