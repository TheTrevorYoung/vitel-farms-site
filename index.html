THE OPPORTUNITY

A national crop. A measurable commercial challenge.

Cassava already has national scale in Sierra Leone. The opportunity is to organize production more effectively, improve yields, strengthen access to clean planting material and agronomy, connect growers to dependable markets, and process where the economics work.

Vitel’s role is to build and test that system with evidence. The planned first field focus is northern Sierra Leone, subject to current land, farmer and operating verification before public scale claims are made.

THE OPERATING SYSTEM

Verify. Produce. Process. Scale.

Four stages. No shortcuts.

01

Verify

Confirm land, farmer activity, acreage, production history, costs, buyers and market requirements before making claims or commitments.

02

Produce

Build repeatable field systems around planting material, agronomy, crop calendars, measured yields, harvest planning and cost control.

03

Process

Test gari and other value-added pathways against demand, quality specifications, conversion rates, capital needs and margins.

04

Scale

Expand the farmer network, production, processing and market channels only where demonstrated performance supports growth.

THE FARMER NETWORK

One nucleus. A distributed production network.

Vitel’s model is not based on owning vast tracts of land. It is designed around a nucleus-farm function and a progressively verified network of independent farmers, farmer groups and future outgrowers—linked by data, agronomy, planting material, aggregation and markets.
