CROP PORTFOLIO

One farm. Multiple crop pathways.

Vitel Farms is the enduring platform. Individual crops enter, grow or leave the portfolio according to evidence.

THE PORTFOLIO RULE

Each crop has to earn its place.

Vitel does not expand simply because a crop is popular.

Land & agronomy

Can the crop perform reliably in the intended location and production system?

Market demand

Who buys it, in what form, at what volume, quality, timing and price?

Operating fit

Can Vitel and participating producers manage planting, harvest, handling and logistics well?

Economics

Do costs, losses, processing requirements and margins justify expansion?

CURRENT WORKSTREAM

Cassava

Cassava remains an important Vitel workstream because of its national reach, market pathways and value-addition potential. Vitel also retains a long-horizon ambition to contribute to stronger Sierra Leonean cassava performance.

That ambition belongs to the cassava workstream. It does not define Vitel Farms as a one-crop company.

FUTURE CROPS

Leave room to grow intelligently.

Vitel expects its crop portfolio to expand over time. New crops will be added only when the land, market, operating and economic case is strong enough to support a real decision.

Until that case exists, Vitel will not present a prospective crop as an active program.

THE COMPANY ABOVE THE CROP

Vitel Farms should outlast any single commodity cycle.

The durable asset is the farm system: land knowledge, producer relationships, field discipline, data, logistics, processing judgment and market access. Crops use that system; they do not replace it.
