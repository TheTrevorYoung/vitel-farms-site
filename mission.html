CURRENT FLAGSHIP MISSION

From #22 to #1.

Vitel’s concentrated operating mission today is to help make Sierra Leone the world’s leading cassava-producing country—through measurable improvements in production, farmer organization, market access and value addition.

THE BASELINE

Start with the scoreboard, not the slogan.

Vitel’s current benchmark uses the FAOSTAT 2024 cassava production series for international comparison and Sierra Leone’s 2024 Annual Agricultural Survey for the richer national operating baseline.

The comparable FAO series places Sierra Leone at roughly #22 globally, with production around 2.86 million tonnes. That is meaningful scale—but still far from the global leaders.

THE RACE IS NOT JUST ACREAGE

Three engines of national production.

01

Yield

Better planting material, agronomy, crop health, planting density, weed management, harvest timing and field records can increase production per hectare.

02

Area

More planted and successfully harvested cassava can add volume—but expansion should follow markets, labor capacity and land realities rather than acreage targets alone.

03

Loss reduction & value

Better harvest coordination, aggregation, transport and processing can reduce waste and improve the commercial return on each tonne produced.

LONG HORIZON

Number one is a systems goal.

Nigeria—the current global leader—produces many times Sierra Leone’s volume. Reaching #1 cannot be achieved by one farm, one processor or one project. It requires a national ecosystem of farmers, planting-material systems, research, extension, finance, logistics, processors, markets and public institutions.

Vitel’s ambition is to become one useful commercial platform inside that larger national system. Cassava is where the company is proving that discipline now; future crop expansion should come only after the current mission has demonstrated real capability and results.
