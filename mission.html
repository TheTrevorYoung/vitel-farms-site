ROADMAP

Build what can work now. Earn what comes next.

Two farm projects. One rule: do not scale ahead of supply, buyers and economics.

KAMBIA · CASSAVA

Gari first.
Then build outward.

PHASE 1 · NOW

Gari

Use the five-acre Kambia site as the processing hub. Acquire equipment, organize root supply, process, package and sell.

PHASE 2 · NEXT

HQCF

Add high-quality cassava flour only when buyer specifications, drying, milling, quality control and supply can support it.

PHASE 3 · LONG TERM

Ethanol

Keep cassava ethanol as a long-term industrial option—not an automatic next step. It requires major feedstock, infrastructure, offtake and capital.

THE GATE

Ethanol only happens if the numbers work.

No date is promised. Vitel would first need dependable large-scale cassava supply, proven markets, infrastructure, regulatory clearance and a bankable feasibility case.
