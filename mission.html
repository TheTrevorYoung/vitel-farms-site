CURRENT CASSAVA MISSION

Build the next measurable gain.

Sierra Leone produced about 2.86 million tonnes of cassava in 2024, around #21 globally. Vitel’s long-horizon ambition is to help the country become a world-leading producer. The immediate work is measurable: stronger production, farmer coordination, markets and value addition.

THE 2024 BASELINE

Put the source beside the ambition.

The international baseline uses FAOSTAT’s 2024 cassava production series. It reports Sierra Leone at about 2.86 million tonnes; current FAOSTAT-based rankings place the country around #21 globally.

Nigeria, the current leader, produced about 62.6 million tonnes in the same series. That gap is why #1 belongs in the long-horizon ambition—not as a near-term forecast.

THE NATIONAL SYSTEM

Three engines of cassava growth.

01

Productivity

Better planting material, agronomy, crop health, timing and field records can strengthen production per hectare.

02

Reliable production

Area and output should expand only where labor, land, planting material and markets can support them.

03

Value & loss reduction

Aggregation, transport, processing and stronger markets can improve the return on each tonne produced.

LONG HORIZON

World leadership is a national systems ambition.

No single farm or company can close a gap of this scale. Progress requires farmers, planting-material systems, research, extension, finance, logistics, processors, buyers and public institutions.

Vitel’s role is narrower and more accountable: become one useful commercial platform inside that larger system and report its own contribution separately.
