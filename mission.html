FARM STRATEGY

Build Vitel Farms one proven system at a time.

The primary mission is a durable Sierra Leonean farm and agribusiness platform. Crop strategies sit inside that mission; no single crop defines it.

WHAT VITEL IS BUILDING

A farm system that can grow beyond one crop.

Production base

Land, agronomy, crop planning, field records and disciplined cost control.

Producer network

Verified farmers and groups connected to useful production and market pathways.

Market system

Buyer requirements, logistics, pricing and payment information that shape what gets grown.

Value addition

Processing and post-harvest investment only where demand and economics support it.

HOW A CROP ENTERS THE PORTFOLIO

Four gates before scale.

Land fit

Can the crop perform reliably in the intended geography?

Demand

Is there a real market with a clear product, volume, quality and timing requirement?

Operating fit

Can Vitel and participating producers grow, harvest, handle and move it well?

Economics

Do costs, losses, processing needs and margins support a sustainable business?
