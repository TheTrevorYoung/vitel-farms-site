WORK WITH VITEL

Tell us where you fit in the cassava system.

We are building distinct pathways for farmers, buyers/processors and technical or program partners.

01

Farmers & producer groups

For cassava farmers, cooperatives and FBOs interested in production support, planting-material pathways, demonstration activity, aggregation or future market linkage.

  • Where do you farm?
  • How much cassava are you currently growing?
  • When is your crop planted or expected to be harvested?
  • Who do you currently sell to?
  • What is the biggest constraint?
02

Buyers & processors

For buyers of roots, leaves, gari, flour, starch or other cassava products, and processors seeking a stronger supply pipeline.

  • What product do you buy?
  • What volume and frequency?
  • What quality specification?
  • Farmgate or delivered?
  • What are the payment terms?
03

Technical, program & capital partners

For institutions working on agronomy, planting material, farmer organization, processing, finance, logistics, research or market development.

  • What capability or program do you operate?
  • Which districts and beneficiaries are eligible?
  • What requirements must Vitel or farmers meet?
  • Where could a northern farmer network add value?
