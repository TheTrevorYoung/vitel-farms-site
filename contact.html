WORK WITH VITEL

Start with what you grow, buy or support.

Choose the pathway that fits you. Vitel is building a broader farm and agribusiness platform, not a one-crop program.

01

Farmers & producer groups

Start with five simple facts.

  • Where do you farm?
  • What crops do you grow?
  • About how much land or production do you manage?
  • When do you expect to harvest?
  • What is your biggest constraint?
02

Buyers & processors

Tell us the requirement. Vitel uses it to shape crop and production decisions.

  • What crop or product do you buy?
  • What volume and frequency?
  • What quality specification?
  • What delivery window and location?
  • What are the payment terms?
03

Technical, program & capital partners

We are interested in capabilities that strengthen production, processing, finance, logistics or markets.

  • What capability or program do you operate?
  • Where do you work?
  • What eligibility or operating requirements apply?
  • Where could a northern producer network add value?
