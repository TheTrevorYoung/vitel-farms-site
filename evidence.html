EVIDENCE & METHODOLOGY

Know what is measured, inferred and still being verified.

Vitel treats credibility as operating infrastructure.

PUBLIC CLAIM STANDARD

Every important claim belongs in a category.

Documented: supported by a current authoritative source or reliable internal record appropriate to the claim.

Owner-confirmed: supplied directly by Vitel leadership but still awaiting independent documentary or field verification where that matters.

Pending verification: historical, third-party or incomplete information that cannot yet be treated as a current fact.

Target / ambition: a future objective, explicitly written as such.

BASELINE RECONCILIATION

One figure can have two legitimate source roles.

The national survey is the primary Sierra Leone operating baseline for the cassava workstream. It reports about 2.86 million tonnes alongside planted area, harvested area and yield.

FAOSTAT reports a comparable 2024 production figure and is used for the international ranking.

The 11.8 t/ha yield is interpreted against the survey’s 242,380 ha harvested area. It should not be multiplied by the larger 262,326 ha planted-area figure.

RANKING METHOD

Why the cassava page says “around #21.”

The international position is calculated by sorting countries in the same FAOSTAT 2024 cassava production series. Current FAOSTAT-based rankings place Sierra Leone around #21 globally, with production around 2.86 million tonnes.

Vitel uses “around” because source revisions and ranking methods can produce small differences.

WHAT WE WILL NOT DO

Legacy evidence does not silently become a current claim.

Historical farmer relationships, land references, processing concepts, equipment, partnerships and project documents are useful evidence of institutional history. They do not prove that the same assets, relationships or operating conditions exist in 2026.

Current claims require current verification.

CORRECTIONS

If the evidence changes, the site should change.

Vitel’s public evidence will be updated when authoritative statistics are revised or current field verification materially changes an operating fact.
