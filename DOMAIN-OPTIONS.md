# Domain decision — Vitel Farms Sierra Leone

## Recommended order

1. **vitelfarms.sl** — preferred if available. Strong Sierra Leone identity and more distinct from the established U.S. brand “Vital Farms.”
2. **vitelfarms.com.sl** — strong Sierra Leone commercial fallback if the root .sl is unavailable or undesirable.
3. **vitelfarms.africa** — useful regional identity if available.
4. **vitelfarmssl.com** — clear global fallback that explicitly distinguishes the Sierra Leone venture.

## Do not default blindly to vitelfarms.com

“Vitel Farms” is only one letter away from the established U.S. food brand “Vital Farms.” Vital Farms owns active U.S. trademark registrations and has a documented history of enforcing its marks. This note is a launch-risk flag, not a legal conclusion that Vitel Farms infringes any rights.

Before purchasing a primary global domain or expanding into overlapping food categories/markets, consider a proper trademark clearance review in the jurisdictions that matter to the business.

## Registry note

The Sierra Leone .sl registry states that .sl and .com.sl are open for general/commercial registration and that registrations are first-come, first-served. Availability must still be checked at purchase time.
